# REP design

The power machine follows the first approved concept board: a detailed inked blue-and-yellow appliance, red reactor porthole, transparent external pipes, service hatch and a limestone Élysée with slate roofs. The machine is a transparent illustration with registered SVG animation layers, not a WebGL scene. The portraits follow the restrained bust caricatures in the same reference.

The page is midnight #0b1020. Task surfaces use #151d30, text #f3f1e9 and secondary text #b6bfd3. Yellow #ffd52f carries the current mission and primary action. Cyan #42d5ff carries water, adjustable inputs and stability progress. No green or lime. DM Sans is bundled locally.

The machine remains the dominant workspace between tasks. An active task covers its area while preserving the presidents, their dialogue, the five-mission progress and playback controls. Each mission has three phases. Task controls are outside dialogue. Instructions remain visible; errors replace the current instruction with a concrete recovery cue and a presidential response.

The machine's core brightens after startup, the turbines rotate, water moves through a registered pipe path and steam rises from the roof. The palace brightens when all three feeders connect. Pause stops both tasks and illustration motion; quarter speed slows both. Reduced motion stops decorative rotor, fluid and steam movement. Moving task targets are the games themselves and remain visible.

The layout fits a dynamic mobile viewport, with safe-area top and bottom insets. Compact portrait preserves task controls and full dialogue. Short landscape moves dialogue beside the task. Native buttons and ranges support touch and keyboard; focus transfers to the first task control after phase changes and back to the mission action after completion.
